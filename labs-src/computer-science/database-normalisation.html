Guidance: explicit
1NFcurrent

Atomic values; one row per enrolment. Repeated student and course facts are still stored in the relationship table.

2NFnext

Every non-key fact depends on the whole composite key, not only StudentID or CourseID.

3NFlater

Non-key facts do not depend on other non-key facts.
