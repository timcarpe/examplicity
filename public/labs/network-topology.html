Network Topology Builder

For Cambridge AS & A Level Computer Science 9618, build bus, star, mesh and hybrid networks, then send packets through the result.

AS 9618 · 2.1
Goal: Connect at least four stations to one shared backbone. Connect each station once to the terminated backbone; do not link stations directly.
Parts Drag a part onto the workspace, or tap it to add.
Choose Cable, then drag between two parts. You can also select one part and then another.
Start with the parts. Add the required devices, connect them, then check your topology.
Network ready. Choose two stations and send a packet.
Stations send and receive. Switches, bridges and shared buses can carry packets onward. In Hybrid, add direct mesh links over an existing bus or star and compare which route the packet chooses.
