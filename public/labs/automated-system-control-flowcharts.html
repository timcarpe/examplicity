Factory requirement

A correctly filled bottle weighs 490–510 g. A pressure sensor sits under the weighing plate: more liquid produces more bottle weight, so the measured force is calibrated to bottle mass. The sensor reading is analogue and must be converted to digital data before the microprocessor can use it.
