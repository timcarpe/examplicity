# Learner review: proposed, not yet conducted

Use four to six learners from the intended curriculum as a small formative usability review, not an effectiveness study. Work individually, with the same device type learners normally use. Include a learner who needs mathematical support and a confident learner.

## Procedure

Ask the learner to use one guided journey and its final experiment while saying what they are looking at and why they are changing it. Do not explain a control or relationship immediately. Record the point of hesitation and the help they choose. Intervene when they cannot proceed, noting exactly what was supplied.

After the guided journey, ask for one fresh case using the same apparatus and less instruction:

| Lab | Fresh case to observe |
| --- | --- |
| Maths | With original edge 2, explain the distinction between new edge 6 and scale factor 3, then predict an area or volume change. |
| Physics | Predict how changing one medium moves a threshold; test the same boundary in reverse. |
| Gas | Save a reading, vary one condition, then choose readings that test whether the same product still applies. |
| Biology | Start from the same cohort and design a different food supply; predict which frequencies will change before advancing generations. |

## Record only actionable observations

For each difficulty, note the visible state, what the learner tried, what they said the evidence meant, whether help was requested, and whether the next attempt changed their explanation or only their answer. Distinguish locating a handle from choosing a useful manipulation.

Check whether the learner can keep the manipulated object, relevant measurement and prediction in view without losing their place. Inspect phone use separately. Note accidental resets, unreadable units, missed reference markers and instructions that disclose the answer before the learner interprets the model.

Revise a specific cue, comparison or task when the observation supports it. Do not add another panel by default. Completing all checkpoints, finding a green state or spending longer in a lab is not evidence of understanding. Report this small review descriptively, with examples and limitations, not as a measured learning gain.
