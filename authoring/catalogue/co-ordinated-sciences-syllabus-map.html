Method and provenance

The map was generated from a local objective-level research ledger and independently reviewed biology, chemistry and physics mapping datasets. It reports the recorded relationship only; it does not reproduce objective wording. Equivalent means command, content and official Core/Supplement depth align. A compatible extension shares an operated relationship but the aligned syllabus adds scope or depth. Thematic-only records provide related context, not a direct map or shared coverage. No-safe-map records have no conservative objective-level pair in the comparison window.

  • 0654 is the research spine; 0610, 0620 and 0625 are aligned only where the reviewed data records an objective-level relationship.
  • The 0654 syllabus applies to 2025–2027 and the aligned syllabuses apply to 2026–2028, so this comparison is restricted to their shared 2026–2027 period.
  • PDF page numbers are the source PDF pages. Links use the standard #page= fragment supported by most PDF viewers; page numbers remain visible if a viewer ignores the fragment.
  • This artifact contains identifiers, section names, pages and brief mapping rationale—not the syllabus objective text—so it can be shared as an open reference alongside Cambridge’s official documents.
